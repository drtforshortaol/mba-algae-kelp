# Dead man's fingers photo review

The [Aquarium profile](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/dead-mans-fingers) confirms the guide species as *Codium fragile*. The previous subject in [observation 96022275](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/96022275) appeared small in the frame, and the rounded branching form was difficult to see. The image alone did not establish an incorrect observation ID.

Compared 22 openly licensed California candidate photos. [Replacement photo 102829393](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/102829393) from [research-grade observation 64008298](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/64008298) shows rounded branches, repeated forks, and a hand for scale. CC0, resized. Other useful comparisons: [whole specimen on rock](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/33019391) and [thick forks in hand](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/104577575).
