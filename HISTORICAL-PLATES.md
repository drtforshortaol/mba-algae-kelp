# Historical plates and habitat photo

Dawson’s plate shows type material published as R. lobulifera, treated by the source as a synonym of R. pacifica. Taylor's plate shows Fauchea galapagensis, the original combination for Gloiocladia galapagensis. The Ulva prolifera photo shows a shoreline bloom and does not resolve individual branches. Images were resized for offline use.

- **Pacific rose seaweed** (*Rhodymenia pacifica*) — [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plate_25_%28Dawson%2C_1941%29.jpg), E. Y. Dawson, 1941, plate 25, figure 36, [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0).
- **Gloiocladia galapagensis** (*Gloiocladia galapagensis*) — [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plate_82_%28Taylor%2C_1945%29.jpg), W. R. Taylor, 1945, plate 82, [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0).
- **Branched string lettuce** (*Ulva prolifera*) — [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coastalprolifera.jpg), CathayanBoris, [license](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en).
