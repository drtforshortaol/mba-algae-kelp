# Final visual references

- [Cryptopleura lobulifera](https://file.iflora.cn/fastdfs/group2/M00/64/C8/wKhnol2BDwyAMLtWALy9LE5ARr0361.pdf): original, simplified schematic based on Dawson's description and plate 46, figure 4. The branched, ruffled blade and lower midrib are visible in the drawing. Microscopy may still be needed.
- [Mazzaella rosea](https://symbiota.birdmap.africa/collections/individual/index.php?occid=264540): Bolus Herbarium specimen BOL0262411, University of Cape Town, CC BY 4.0, cropped and resized. The specimen was filed as Rhodoglossum roseum, [a synonym](https://www.snib.mx/taxonomia/checklist/taxon/?id=10793). Pressed appearance should not be used to confirm species alone.
