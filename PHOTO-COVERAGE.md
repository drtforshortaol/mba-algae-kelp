# Identification image coverage

The current offline guide includes seven photographs. Six are attached to matching directory entries. The red coralline growth-form comparison is identified explicitly as a separate example.

Added photographs: [feather boa kelp, Therese Phillips, CC BY 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Egregia_menziesii_-_Therese_Phillips_-_708215618.jpeg), [rockweed, jkirkhart35, CC BY 2.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fucusdistichus.jpg), and [Turkish towel, Fitzgerald Marine Reserve, CC BY 2.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turkish_Towel_(Chondracanthus_exasperatus)_01.jpg). All were resized for offline use.

Each directory entry without a matching photo identifies the gap in its expanded details. Prioritize images of Monterey-area specimens, confirm the taxon and reusable license, and pair images with distinguishing shape, texture, and habitat. A realistic illustration should be based on verified source images and labelled as an illustration.
