# Anatomy references

- *Dictyoneurum reticulatum*: original schematic of the blade and central midrib, based on [published specimen comparisons](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Sporophyte-morphology-of-selected-agaracean-species-a-and-b-Type-of-Agarum-oharaense_fig1_312362097). Corrected the directory spelling.
- Dwarf skein (*Antithamnion defectum*): original schematic of paired comb-like branchlets and an occasional unpaired longer branch, based on [Seaweeds of Alaska](https://www.seaweedsofalaska.com/species/rhodophyta/florideophyceae/ceramiales/ceramiaceae/antithamnion/defectum).
- Both are simplified and out of scale. Seven entries still need a defensible visual: Feldmannia mitchelliae, Lychaete sakaii, arched red seaweed, criss-cross network, Cryptopleura lobulifera, Mazzaella rosea, Pugetia fragilissima.
