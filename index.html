Identify a seaweed

Start with shape, texture, and habitat. Color is one clue and can vary within each group.

Brown · Algas pardas

Fucoxanthin often gives these mostly marine algae an olive or brown color. Look for leathery blades and floats on some kelps.

Red · Algas rojas

Reds can look pink, purple, or iridescent. Look for forms ranging from delicate branches to hard coralline crusts, from tide pools to deeper sunlit water.

Green · Algas verdes

Chlorophyll gives many a grass-green color. Compare the thin sheets of sea lettuce with the dark, spongy forms of some Codium.

Answer a guest question

Does kelp have roots?

A holdfast grips the rock like an anchor. Kelp takes up dissolved nutrients from surrounding seawater.

At the Kelp Touch Pool, try asking “Which part helps kelp stay attached when a wave comes through?”

What’s inside the kelp floats?

Gas gives the floats, called pneumatocysts, their buoyancy. The floats lift blades closer to sunlight. Giant kelp has many small floats, and bull kelp has one large float at the top of its stipe.

What’s the difference between algae, seaweed, and kelp?

Algae is a broad term that includes microscopic organisms. Seaweed means larger marine algae, and kelp is a type of brown seaweed.

Is eelgrass a seaweed?

Eelgrass is a flowering plant adapted to seawater. It has roots and produces flowers and seeds.

Where might I encounter algae at home?

Nori wraps many sushi rolls. Agar and carrageenan come from red algae and can help foods gel or thicken. Alginates from brown algae have similar uses.

Explore ocean changes

Warming and kelp loss

Marine heatwaves can stress kelp. After warming and increased urchin grazing, monitoring showed a loss of over 90% of bull-kelp canopy off Mendocino and Sonoma counties from 2014 to 2016.

Acidification and local pH

Seawater pH falls as the ocean absorbs carbon dioxide. Kelp and eelgrass take up dissolved carbon dioxide during photosynthesis, which can raise pH in nearby water. The size and duration of the effect depend on local conditions.

Ask “How could a meadow change the water flowing through it?”

Oxygen loss

Warmer water can hold less dissolved oxygen. Warming can also raise oxygen demand in animals and microbes. Low oxygen can make it harder for some animals to thrive.

Compare the habitats

Kelp forests grow on rocky seafloors. Their canopy and holdfasts create shelter at different heights.

Eelgrass meadows often grow in shallow sediment. Their blades provide cover for young fish and habitat for small invertebrates.

Algae versus land plants

Seaweeds and land plants both photosynthesize and contain chlorophyll a. Many seaweeds have a holdfast, stipe, and blades. Most familiar land plants have roots, stems, and leaves with specialized transport tissues.

Seaweed life cycles

In many seaweeds, a spore-producing sporophyte (two chromosome sets) alternates with a gamete-producing gametophyte (one set). In kelp, the large form you see is the sporophyte. The gametophytes are microscopic.

Many red algae, including Polysiphonia, have a third stage. After fertilization, a carposporophyte develops on the female gametophyte and releases spores that grow into tetrasporophytes. Those produce spores that grow into new gametophytes.
