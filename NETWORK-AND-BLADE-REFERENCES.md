# Anatomy references

- [Criss-cross network](https://www.seaweedsofalaska.com/species.asp?SeaweedID=260) is *Polyneura latissima*. The original diagram emphasizes interconnected veins.
- [Pugetia fragilissima](https://www.bridgetteclarkston.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Phycol-2012-Clarkston.pdf), Clarkston and Saunders, figures 5–10. The original diagram summarizes one blade form and notes the limits of visual identification.
- [Lychaete sakaii](https://www.kitasato-u.ac.jp/mb/albums/abm.php?d=532&f=abm00003728.pdf), Kitasato University page 8, published as *Cladophora sakaii*. The diagram shows the described single row of cylindrical cells and multiple branching modes.

Remaining: *Cryptopleura lobulifera* and *Mazzaella rosea*. Smithsonian candidate records surfaced, but downloadable reusable specimen images could not be verified.
