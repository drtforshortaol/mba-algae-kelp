# Pink coralline algae

[SeaNet](https://seanet.stanford.edu/rocky-shores) describes jointed erect forms and pink crusts. The guide compares an existing articulated specimen photo of Calliarthron cheilosporioides with an encrusting example. The crustose photo is [iNaturalist photo 30390721](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/30390721), [observation 19725796](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/19725796), credited to Javier, CC BY 4.0, cropped and resized. These examples are for identifying growth forms. The user plans to supply station-specific touch-pool photos later.
