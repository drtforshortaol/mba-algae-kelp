# Specimen scans

Scans were cropped to the specimen and resized. Six images show usable structure. Available dwarf skein and Veleroa scans were left pending because the specimen was too small or faint. The coarse sea lace image is a pressed reference labeled unvouchered by its source.

- **Anisocladella pacifica** (*Anisocladella pacifica*) — [source record](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/1318351890), Conveyor Belt, [license](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Coarse sea lace** (*Microcladia borealis*) — [source record](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/2633833051), *unvouchered, [license](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Gymnogongrus platyphyllus** (*Gymnogongrus platyphyllus*) — [source record](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/3897788370), Conveyor Belt, [license](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Herposiphonia plumula** (*Herposiphonia plumula*) — [source record](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/1321392936), Conveyor Belt, [license](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
