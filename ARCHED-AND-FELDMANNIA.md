# Two source-based illustrations

- [Arched red seaweed](https://seaweedcanada.wordpress.com/fryeella-gardneri-setchell-kylin-bc/) (*Fryeella gardneri*): flattened divided blades, short stipe, and arc-like surface markings.
- [Feldmannia mitchelliae](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12328641/): fine filamentous tuft and elongated partitioned sporangia.

Both are original schematics and labelled as out of scale.

Remaining: Lychaete sakaii, criss-cross network (common name unresolved), Cryptopleura lobulifera, Mazzaella rosea, and Pugetia fragilissima.
